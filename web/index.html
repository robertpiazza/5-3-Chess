How Pieces Move
5³ Chess is played on a 5×5×5 board — pieces move in full 3D space.
PieceMovement
Rook Slides any distance along one axis — X, Y, or Z (6 directions)
Bishop Slides along face diagonals — exactly 2 axes change together (12 directions)
Unicorn Slides along space diagonals — all 3 axes change together (8 directions)
Queen Combines Rook + Bishop + Unicorn — slides in all 26 directions
King One step in any of the 26 directions — must stay out of check
Knight L-shape in 3D: 2 squares on one axis + 1 on another; jumps over pieces (up to 24 moves)
Pawn Advances one layer forward (White: +Z, Black: −Z); captures one step diagonally forward; promotes on the far layer
