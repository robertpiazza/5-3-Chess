Product Requirements Document – Dimension Chess VR (working title)

1. Executive Summary

Dimension Chess VR is a Meta Quest app that brings 5 × 5 × 5 three‑dimensional chess (based on the historical Raumschach rule‑set) into an immersive multiplayer environment. The goal is to create a comfortable, engaging, and competitive experience that appeals to strategy gamers, chess enthusiasts looking for a fresh challenge, and VR early adopters.

2. Objectives & Success Metrics

Primary objective: Ship a polished VR chess variant that reaches a positive user rating of 4.4 +/5 on the Meta Quest Store within 90 days of launch.

Engagement: Average session length ≥ 15 min, 30‑day retention ≥ 25 %.

Multiplayer adoption: ≥ 60 % of weekly active users play at least one online match per week.

Revenue: Break‑even on development cost within 9 months via paid downloads + cosmetic DLC.

3. Target Audience / Personas

Core Strategist (Alex, 32): Enjoys classic and variant chess, owns a Quest 3, looks for deep competitive play.

Social Gamer (Jordan, 27): Uses VR to connect with friends, values voice chat and quick matchmaking.

Learner (Sam, 18): Attracted by novel board games, needs robust tutorials and AI sparring partners.

4. Use Cases / User Stories

As a new player I can take an interactive tutorial so I understand how pieces move in 3‑D.

As a competitor I can create or join ranked matches with ELO‑style ratings.

As a casual player I can invite a friend for a private game with voice chat.

As a learner I can play against AI of adjustable difficulty and request in‑game hints.

As a spectator I can watch ongoing public matches in an arena‑style view.

6. Non‑Functional Requirements

Performance: Maintain 90 FPS on Quest 3, 72 FPS on Quest 2.

Comfort: Seated and standing modes, no artificial locomotion, minimal motion sickness risk.

Accessibility: Color‑blind palettes, adjustable board scale, subtitles for chat.

Security & Privacy: Meta entitlement checks, encrypted match data, COPPA compliance.

7. UX / UI Requirements

Layer toggle and slice view to examine board levels.

Hand and controller support, option for hand tracking v2 at launch + haptics.

Lobby UI with quick play, ranked, tutorial, and custom match tiles.

Contextual audio cues for piece moves, check, and capture.

Calm, sci‑fi aesthetic backdrop with focus on board legibility.

8. Technical Architecture

Engine: Unity 2023 LTS with Meta XR SDK.

Networking: Photon Fusion (tick‑rate 30 Hz) or Meta’s RR2 transport if first‑party requirement emerges.

AI: Stockfish‑derived engine extended to 5 × 5 × 5 bitboard logic; compute on device.

Data: Matchmaking, ELO, cosmetics stored in Firebase.

Build pipeline: GitHub Actions – CI, Quest Performance Tester, automated store builds.

9. Monetization & Pricing

Base price: USD 14.99 one‑time purchase.

DLC: Cosmetic boards/pieces USD 2.99–4.99; season pass for themed sets every 6 months.

No ads, no consumables.

11. Success Metrics (detailed)

Daily Active Users (DAU), Weekly Multiplayer Games, Tutorial Completion Rate, Average Match Duration, User Rating, Paid DLC attach rate.

12. Risks & Mitigations

Rule complexity deters newcomers – mitigate with layered tutorials, assistive move previews.

VR comfort issues – strictly stationary play space, customizable board distance.

Server costs spike with success – auto‑scaling Photon tier and DLC revenue allocation.

AI performance on mobile hardware – use compiled C++ engine and fallback heuristic layer.

13. Future Enhancements

Hand‑tracked piece manipulation.

Cross‑play with PC VR and mixed‑reality tabletop mode.

Tournament brackets and seasonal leaderboards.

SDK plugin for custom piece sets by modders.
